Why this matters

Asking a mint whether proofs are spent sends it identifiers (Y) derived from your proof secrets. The answer is only the mint's own word at one moment in time, and proof state is only checked when you turn this on.

Ask the mint for proof state?

Asking a mint whether proofs are spent sends it identifiers (Y) derived from your proof secrets, so the mint learns which proofs you hold. The answer is only the mint's own word at one moment in time.
